RESOLUTION GUIDE
═════════════════

512x512 pixels
├─ Size: ~50KB
├─ Tokens: ~85
├─ Cost: Low
└─ Use for: Quick checks, grasp verification

768x768 pixels ⭐ RECOMMENDED
├─ Size: ~100KB
├─ Tokens: ~170
├─ Cost: Medium
└─ Use for: Most commands, normal operation

1024x1024 pixels
├─ Size: ~200KB
├─ Tokens: ~255
├─ Cost: High
└─ Use for: Detailed analysis, complex boards

Original (1280x720)
├─ Size: ~300KB
├─ Tokens: ~340
├─ Cost: Very High
└─ Use for: Special cases only
